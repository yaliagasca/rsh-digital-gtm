B.8 Executive Presentation Readout Structure

Slalom will develop an executive-ready Digital GTM Strategy Readout Presentation designed for RS&H’s ELT, DTS leadership, and other senior decision-makers. The readout will translate complex assessment findings, market analysis, offering strategy, operating model implications, and financial scenarios into a clear leadership decision framework.

The executive readout will be structured to help RS&H make informed decisions on where to invest, how to commercialize, and how to sequence execution. Recommended sections include:

Executive Summary and Strategic Context: A concise summary of why the Digital GTM Strategy matters, how it supports Amplify 2030, and what decisions are required from leadership.

Current-State Validation Findings: Key findings from RS&H’s digital capability assessment, including strengths, gaps, commercialization barriers, operating model implications, and readiness to scale.

Market Opportunity and Demand Validation: Summary of TAM/SAM/SOM findings, buyer demand, market attractiveness, competitive positioning, and priority opportunities across Transportation, Aviation, and Federal Defense.

Prioritized Digital Portfolio: Recommended digital advisory, managed services, and product/platform-enabled offerings, including client problems, target buyers, value propositions, delivery models, pricing considerations, and required capabilities.

Commercialization and GTM Strategy: Recommended sales motions, account-based growth strategy, bundled versus standalone positioning, partner/alliance considerations, pursuit integration, and enablement requirements.

Operating Model and Governance Implications: Recommended decision rights, ownership model, governance forums, talent needs, delivery model considerations, and cross-functional alignment requirements.

Business and Financial Model: Revenue-model options, pricing and margin assumptions, investment requirements, near-, mid-, and long-term growth scenarios, and key financial tradeoffs.

Recommended Roadmap and Decision Points: Sequenced recommendations for near-term activation, future scaling, leadership decisions, and implementation next steps.

Appendix / Supporting Detail: Detailed market findings, scoring criteria, financial assumptions, stakeholder inputs, and supporting analysis.

All final materials will be delivered in editable RS&H-owned formats so DTS and RS&H leadership can continue to use them for internal alignment, board-level communication, implementation planning, and future portfolio decisions.
