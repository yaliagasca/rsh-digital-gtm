B.6 Knowledge-Transfer Plan

Knowledge transfer will be embedded throughout the engagement through co-creation, working sessions, and iterative review—not treated as a final handoff activity. RS&H stakeholders will participate directly in capability validation, market interpretation, offering prioritization, financial modeling, and GTM design so that the rationale behind the strategy is understood as it is developed.

At engagement close, Slalom will provide a structured knowledge-transfer and handoff package that includes:

Core-team working session: At least one dedicated session with the RS&H core team covering the Digital GTM Strategy, financial model, and strategic roadmap in sufficient depth for RS&H leaders to present, explain, and defend the recommendations internally.

Methods and data-source guide: Documentation of the key analytical methods, assumptions, scoring criteria, research sources, and decision logic used to develop the market assessment, portfolio prioritization, and recommendations.

Financial-model handoff: An editable financial model with documented assumptions, scenarios, calculation logic, and guidance for updating the model as actual performance and market conditions change.

Reusable strategy tools: Editable portfolio-assessment criteria, prioritization frameworks, GTM decision logic, and supporting artifacts that RS&H can reuse as new opportunities emerge.

Editable final deliverables: Strategy, models, analysis, and presentation materials provided in RS&H-owned formats for continued use and adaptation.

The VP of Digital Technologies & Solutions will serve as the primary post-engagement owner, with relevant DTS, Business Development, Operational Business Unit, and other core-team participants included in working sessions and handoff activities based on their responsibilities.

The objective is for RS&H to own not only the recommendations, but also the methods, assumptions, and tools behind them—allowing DTS and BD to operate against a common digital GTM playbook and continue evolving the strategy independently.

B.7 Executive Presentation Readout Structure

Slalom will develop an executive-ready Digital GTM Strategy Readout Presentation for an in-person presentation to RS&H’s Executive Leadership Team in Jacksonville, supported by a working-session readout with the core project team. The readout will translate complex assessment findings, market analysis, offering strategy, operating model implications, and financial scenarios into a clear leadership decision framework.

The executive readout will be structured to help RS&H make informed decisions on where to invest, how to commercialize, and how to sequence execution. Recommended sections include:

Executive Summary and Strategic Context: A concise summary of why the Digital GTM Strategy matters, how it supports Amplify 2030, and what decisions are required from leadership.

Current-State Validation Findings: Key findings from RS&H’s digital capability assessment, including strengths, gaps, commercialization barriers, operating model implications, and readiness to scale.

Market Opportunity and Demand Validation: Summary of TAM/SAM/SOM findings, buyer demand, market attractiveness, competitive positioning, and priority opportunities across Transportation, Aviation, and Federal Defense.

Prioritized Digital Portfolio: Recommended digital advisory, managed services, and product/platform-enabled offerings, including client problems, target buyers, value propositions, delivery models, pricing considerations, and required capabilities.

Commercialization and GTM Strategy: Recommended sales motions, account-based growth strategy, bundled-versus-standalone positioning, partner/alliance considerations, pursuit integration, and enablement requirements.

Operating Model and Governance Implications: Recommended decision rights, ownership model, governance forums, talent needs, delivery model considerations, and cross-functional alignment requirements.

Business and Financial Model: Revenue-model options, pricing and margin assumptions, investment requirements, near-, mid-, and long-term growth scenarios, and key financial tradeoffs.

Strategic Roadmap and Decision Points: Prioritized sequencing through FY30, leadership decisions, route-to-market and investment-mode recommendations, ROM investment requirements, key dependencies, and high-level implementation considerations. Detailed implementation planning remains outside the scope of this engagement.

Appendix / Supporting Detail: Detailed market findings, scoring criteria, financial assumptions, stakeholder inputs, and supporting analysis.

The readout will culminate in a concise set of ELT decisions: which offerings RS&H should prioritize; which markets and buyers to pursue first; whether each opportunity should be built, hired, partnered, or acquired; the appropriate route-to-market and commercial model; the level and timing of investment required; and the strategic sequencing needed to advance toward RS&H's FY28 and FY30 growth objectives.

All final materials will be delivered in editable RS&H-owned formats so DTS and RS&H leadership can continue to use them for internal alignment, executive communication, implementation planning, and future portfolio decisions.
