A note from our team

Dear Ms. Chambers and Evaluation Team,

On behalf of Slalom, thank you for the opportunity to respond to RS&H's Digital Go-to-Market Strategy RFP. Amplify 2030 asks DTS to grow digital NSR from roughly $1M to $35M by FY30. You are not asking for a theoretical strategy. You already have the capability across AI/ML, data, BIM/VDC, GIS, and digital twins. You are asking a commercialization question: which capabilities become offerings, who buys them, and where to invest first. That is precisely what we do, and our differentiator is the approach, not deliverables anyone can produce. We confirm our intent to perform as scoped, acceptance of the RFP and July 31, 2026 Q&A terms, and 120-day pricing validity.

We understand AEC and asset-intensive businesses. Our transportation and aviation portfolio spans state DOTs, transit, toll and port authorities, airports, and major carriers, built over 20 years and more than 750 public sector engagements. We know what will actually sell in your three markets.

We have commercialized digital capabilities into offerings and revenue. We have helped global engineering, rail, and automotive leaders turn scattered technical capability into productized offerings, pricing, and revenue their own teams now run. We do not theorize about commercialization. We have done it.

We differentiate on approach, connecting market demand, offering design, GTM, operating model, and financials into one logic. Anyone can build a business model. Few can trace the thread that makes it credible, and that is where value is won or lost. Our approach has linked market sizing to billion-dollar opportunities and platform strategy to triple-digit ROI. For RS&H, it delivers a prioritized portfolio, a GTM playbook, and a financial model your teams can stand behind as one strategy, not five disconnected workstreams.

Should Slalom be selected, RS&H gains a partner distinguished by approach, delivering the full scope as one accountable team, and transferring the methods, models, and tools so DTS and Business Development can run and evolve the strategy independently.

Thank you for your consideration. We look forward to helping RS&H turn its digital capability into repeatable, high-margin growth. Debbie Casey is your primary point of contact, authorized to represent Slalom on this submission.

Sincerely,

Dan Okray, Managing Director
Slalom, Inc.
