B.2.2 Capability Assessment Methodology

The first thing required for this assessment is to anchor on how RS&H defines its digital offerings and understand the scope and parameters of the digital assessment. Then the digital capability assessment is organized into three components: 1) inventorying digital offerings and supporting capabilities, 2) assessing current-state maturity effectiveness, and 3) identifying capability gaps and opportunities for improvement. Together, these activities create a fact-based understanding of the organization's digital landscape, how effectively capabilities support customers and business objectives, and where the greatest opportunities exist to improve performance, scalability, and commercial outcomes. The assessment evaluates technology in the context of the broader operating environment, including processes, governance, measurement practices, organizational alignment, and customer-facing activities.

1. Digital Offering & Capability Inventory

Slalom begins by establishing a comprehensive inventory of digital offerings, supporting business capabilities, enabling technologies, data assets, governance structures, and performance measures.

This work also identifies capability ownership, interdependencies, areas of overlap, and supporting systems across the organization. The result is a consolidated view of existing capabilities that serves as the foundation for subsequent assessment activities.

2. Current-State Maturity Assessment

Once capabilities have been inventoried, Slalom evaluates their maturity and effectiveness across people, process, technology, governance, and measurement dimensions. The assessment examines how consistently capabilities are executed, the degree to which they support business objectives, and how effectively enabling technologies and operating practices work together. The maturity assessment combines stakeholder feedback, process analysis, technology reviews, and performance measurement evaluation to identify strengths and areas requiring improvement. Particular focus is given to capabilities that influence customer experience, operational performance, service delivery, and commercial effectiveness.

3. Gap & Opportunity Analysis

Assessment findings are synthesized to identify strengths, pain points, capability gaps, operational inefficiencies, technology constraints, and organizational dependencies. Analysis focuses on both individual capability performance and the interaction points between teams, processes, technologies, and governance structures.

This phase also evaluates the business impact of identified gaps, including potential effects on customer experience, employee productivity, revenue generation, scalability, and operational efficiency. The output provides a clear view of where opportunities exist to strengthen capabilities and improve business outcomes.

Example Outputs: Session summaries and decision logs, pain point inventory, Partner customer feedback, effectiveness evaluation across design and engineering, build, sell, and support (covering digital capability inventory, demand signals, operating-model gaps, pricing barriers, and talent gaps), capability maturity heatmap highlighting strengths, gaps and maturity levels of assessed capability based on industry standard and RS&H future state ambition, customer journey and lifecycle visualization based on capability assessment findings.
